About Us

Built on Trust & Excellence

Established in 2026, NAVYA KRISHNA INFRA is an independent residential house construction company based in Andhra Pradesh, India. We do not build commercial complexes or apartment towers—our entire focus is strictly dedicated to constructing custom, long-lasting independent houses for families.

Quality

We use tested, high-grade materials and sound engineering practices to construct durable independent homes that endure for generations.

Honesty

Built on complete transparency, clear budgeting, and direct communication throughout every stage of house construction.

Strong Construction

Uncompromised structural engineering, rigorous load planning, certified cement and steel to guarantee total safety.

Customer Satisfaction

Dedicated to listening to homeowners' exact needs and creating comfortable, personalized, long-lasting living spaces.

What We Offer

Our Specialized Services

We specialize strictly in independent residential housing construction services.

Independent House Construction

Complete end-to-end turnkey construction of independent residential houses from foundation excavation to final painting.

Construction Consultation

Professional guidance on material selection, estimation, government approvals, and site evaluation before you build.

Structural Planning

Comprehensive column, beam, foundation, and structural load calculations to ensure absolute safety and longevity.
